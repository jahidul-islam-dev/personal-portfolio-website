About Me

Understanding my journey, values, and what drives my growth as a developer

I'm a Computer Science student actively learning and building in the Python and DevOps space. I started my coding journey with a passion for problem-solving and have been exploring backend development, automation, and infrastructure concepts along the way.

Recently, I've been diving deeper into Docker, Linux, and cloud fundamentals. I'm also interested in exploring VLSI Physical Design (PnR) as a secondary learning area. I contribute to open-source projects and enjoy learning from the community.
